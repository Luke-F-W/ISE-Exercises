Lab Exercise: Wealth & Age Calculator

Objective: Practice reading standard input, choosing appropriate primitive data types, performing basic arithmetic operations, and calculating power increments.

Exercise Requirements

Write a Java program that collects a user's details and projects their age and financial growth at retirement age (assume retirement age is 67).

    Prompt for Input:
        User's full name (Text string)
        User's current year of birth (Whole number, e.g., 2005)
        User's current net worth in Euros and cents (Decimal number, e.g., 1250.75)
    Perform Calculations:
        Current Age: Subtract the birth year from the current year ().
        Years to Retirement: Subtract current age from .
        Retirement Wealth: Calculate expected wealth assuming net worth doubles every single year until retirement.

    Output Summary: Display a summary showing their calculated current age, years left until retirement, and estimated retirement wealth formatted to two decimal places.

Expected Output Example

Plaintext

Enter your full name: Alex Smith

Enter your birth year: 2005

Enter your net worth in Euros (€): 1500.50

 

--- Summary for Alex Smith ---

Current Age: 21

Years to Retirement (age 67): 46

Projected Wealth at Retirement: €105,584,242,504,564,736.00
